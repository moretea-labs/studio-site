VEILSPACE · PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Veilspace is a Chrome new-tab extension for user-managed website shortcuts, searches and optional lightweight widgets. Your settings and shortcuts are stored locally in Chrome.

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Information stored on your device

Workspace names, shortcut titles and URLs, link ordering, language, appearance settings, clock size and placement, enabled widgets and their placement, and quick-note text are stored using Chrome extension-local storage. Optional wallpaper images are resized and converted to JPEG locally before storage. The developer does not receive this data.

Saving the current page

When you explicitly open the Veilspace toolbar button, Chrome's temporary activeTab permission allows the extension to read the active page's title and URL so you can save it as a shortcut. The page is saved locally only after you choose a workspace and confirm Save. Veilspace does not continuously inspect tabs or collect browsing history or page content.

Searches and website visits

When you submit a search, Chrome sends the query to its configured default search provider or to the destination you explicitly choose. Opening a shortcut makes a normal browser request to that website. Those services handle your activity under their own privacy policies. Veilspace does not send search queries or browsing activity to the developer.

Optional widgets

The quick note is stored locally. The focus timer runs in the open new-tab page; its countdown is not sent to a server or retained as an activity history.

Permissions

  • storage: save your shortcuts and preferences locally.
  • search: use Chrome's configured search provider when you submit a search.
  • activeTab: read the current page title and URL only after you explicitly open the toolbar action.

The extension does not request browsing-history, bookmarks, broad persistent host access or a backend connection.

Sharing, analytics and advertising

Veilspace has no user accounts, developer backend, analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs or remote executable code. The developer does not sell or receive your extension data.

Retention and control

Your data remains in Chrome until you edit it, reset or clear the extension's data, or uninstall the extension. Exported backups may contain your shortcuts, preferences, notes and optional wallpaper. Keep exported files secure.

Contact and changes

For questions or privacy concerns, use the Veilspace support page. This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

设备本地存储

Veilspace 将空间名称、快捷入口标题和网址、排序、语言、外观设置、时钟设置、小记事内容等保存在 Chrome 扩展本地存储中。自定义壁纸会在本地缩放并转换为 JPEG。开发者不会收到这些数据。

保存当前网页

只有在你主动打开 Veilspace 工具栏按钮后，Chrome 临时授予的 activeTab 权限才允许读取当前页面标题和网址。你选择空间并确认保存后，信息才会写入本地。扩展不会持续读取标签页、浏览历史或网页内容。

搜索与网站访问

提交搜索时，Chrome 会将搜索词发送到浏览器配置的默认搜索引擎，或你主动选择的搜索目标。打开快捷入口会正常访问对应网站。Veilspace 不会将搜索词或浏览活动发送给开发者。

权限

  • storage：在本地保存快捷入口和偏好设置。
  • search：在你提交搜索时调用 Chrome 搜索服务。
  • activeTab：仅在你主动打开工具栏操作后读取当前页面标题和网址。

数据共享与追踪

Veilspace 不提供用户账号或开发者后端，不包含分析、广告、追踪 SDK 或远程可执行代码。开发者不会出售或接收扩展数据。

数据保留与控制

数据保留在 Chrome 中，直到你编辑、重置或清除扩展数据，或卸载扩展。导出的备份可能包含快捷入口、偏好设置、笔记和自定义壁纸，请妥善保管。

联系与政策变更

如有问题或隐私疑虑，请通过Veilspace 支持页面联系。若数据处理方式发生变化，本政策也会相应更新。
