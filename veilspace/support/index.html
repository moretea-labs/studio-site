Support

Need help with Veilspace? Report a problem or ask a question through the public Moretea Lab support tracker.

Open a support request ↗

What to include

  • Your Chrome version and operating system.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue and what you expected to happen.
  • A screenshot only when it helps explain the issue.

Do not include passwords, private URLs, personal health information or other sensitive data in a public report.

Privacy

Read the Veilspace privacy policy to learn what the extension stores and which browser permissions it uses.
